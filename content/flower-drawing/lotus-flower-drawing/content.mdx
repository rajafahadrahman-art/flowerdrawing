## Before You Start Drawing

Before starting your easy lotus flower drawing, remember a few simple ideas:

- The lotus usually looks best when the centre is clear and balanced.
- Inner petals are smaller than the outer petals.
- Petals should open outward in smooth layers.
- The flower should feel symmetrical, but it does not need to be perfectly mechanical.
- Keep your early lines light, then darken the final outline later.

## How to Draw a Lotus Flower Step by Step

This lotus drawing step by step guide follows eight simple stages.
### Step 1: Draw the Centre Guide

Start by sketching a small guide shape near the upper middle of the page. This will help position the flower centre and keep the lotus balanced. Keep this first part light and simple.
### Step 2: Add the First Main Petals

Draw the first visible petals around the centre. These should be upright and slightly pointed at the top. Keep them smooth and evenly spaced.
### Step 3: Build the Inner Lotus Flower

Add more petals behind and beside the first ones. At this point, the flower should begin to look open and layered.
### Step 4: Add the Outer Petal Layer

Now extend the lotus by adding the wider outer petals. These petals help the flower look full and elegant.
### Step 5: Refine the Petal Structure

Improve the edges and shapes of the petals. Make slight adjustments so the bloom looks clean, balanced, and natural.
### Step 6: Draw the Lotus Stem

Add a long, neat stem beneath the flower. The stem should be simple and not too thick.
### Step 7: Add Lotus Leaves

Draw one or more lotus leaves near the stem. Keep them broad and gentle in shape so they match the softness of the flower.
### Step 8: Finish the Lotus Flower Drawing

Darken the final outline, erase unnecessary construction lines, and add light shading if you want a polished finish. This final stage can also become your featured image or worksheet result.
